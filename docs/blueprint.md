# **App Name**: Cultural Artisans Marketplace App

## Core Features:

- Artisan Profile Creation: Allow artisans to create detailed profiles showcasing their craft, region, and story.
- Product Listing with AI Assistance: Provide step-by-step forms for product details, with AI-assisted text generation in multiple Indian languages, to convert product descriptions into polished content and language translations.
- GI Tag Verification: Verify artisans and products using the Government of India’s GI tag database.
- Seller Authenticity Badge: Display a Seller Authenticity Badge, GI Tag Details, and region of origin on product pages.
- Custom Order Requests: Enable buyers to request custom handcrafted orders from artisans, similar to Fiverr’s 'Request a Gig'.
- Secure Payment and Authenticity Checks: Act as a trusted middleman to ensure product authenticity through automated GI tag validation, seller document verification, and random quality checks before the buyer receives the product. Support end-to-end encrypted transactions.
- In-App Chat with Translation: Facilitate direct communication between buyers and sellers with automatic translation to bridge language barriers, functioning as a translation tool that allows them to discuss the finer points of custom work.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Warm terracotta (#E07A5F) to evoke the earthy tones of Indian crafts.
- Background color: Soft beige (#F8F0E3) to provide a clean, neutral canvas.
- Accent color: Rich gold (#D4AF37) for highlights and CTAs to signify the premium nature of the crafts.
- Employ a minimalist layout with clean white space, drawing inspiration from Amazon's product browsing experience but with a subtle Indian aesthetic.
- Body and headline font: 'Alegreya', a serif font that communicates elegance and heritage. Note: currently only Google Fonts are supported.
- Use handcrafted-style icons that evoke traditional Indian art forms.
- Incorporate smooth page transitions and micro-animations for buttons to provide a modern, artisanal feel.
